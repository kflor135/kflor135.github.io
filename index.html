I am currently an undergraduate student at the University of California, Riverside, majoring in Psychology. Throughout my academic journey, I have developed a strong interest in understanding human behavior, mental processes, and the factors that influence individuals' experiences and well-being. I am especially interested in how psychology can be applied to everyday life, education, and healthcare. As I continue my education, I hope to gain experiences that allow me to expand my knowledge, strengthen my professional skills, and make a meaningful impact in my community.

I currently serve as the Vice President of Programming for the Alzheimer's Foundation of America (AFA) at UCR, where I help plan and coordinate events focused on Alzheimer's awareness, education, and community involvement. I work with other student leaders to develop educational programs, organize outreach activities, and collaborate with other campus organizations. This role has helped me strengthen my leadership, communication, teamwork, and organizational skills while contributing to a cause that promotes awareness and understanding of Alzheimer's disease. I also work as a Peer Mentor at UCR's Academic Resource Center (ARC), where I support undergraduate students as they navigate the academic challenges of university life. I help students develop effective study habits, improve time management, set realistic academic goals, and find strategies that work best for their learning styles. Through this experience, I have strengthened my communication and active listening skills while learning the importance of patience, empathy, and encouragement when supporting others. Additionally, I am involved in Psi Chi, the International Honor Society in Psychology, where I have participated in the Psychology Mentorship Program as an undergraduate mentee. Connecting with an alumni mentor has been one of the most rewarding experiences of my undergraduate journey. It has allowed me to learn more about academic and professional opportunities in psychology and inspired me to become more involved in mentorship.

Overall, I am passionate about learning, personal growth, and helping others. My experiences in student leadership, peer mentorship, and psychology have shaped my interest in making a positive difference in people's lives. As I continue working toward my academic and professional goals, I hope to pursue opportunities that allow me to combine my interest in psychology with my desire to support others and contribute to my community.
